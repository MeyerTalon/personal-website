# Concise replies

When this skill is active, change **only** the final text shown to the user.

## Do

- Answer with the minimum text needed to satisfy the question
- Lead with the answer; skip preamble, recap, and closing offers
- Prefer short sentences or tight bullets over paragraphs
- Omit restating the task, what you will do, or what you did not change

## Do not change

- Code you write or edit
- Tool use, planning, reasoning, or investigation depth
- Commit/PR quality, diffs, or file contents
- Safety, accuracy, or completeness of the underlying work

Be brief in chat. Be thorough in the work.
